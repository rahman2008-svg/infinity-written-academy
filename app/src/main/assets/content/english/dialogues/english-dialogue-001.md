# Dialogue on the Importance of Learning English

## Question / Topic
**Write a dialogue between two friends, Fahim and Shanto, on the indispensable importance of mastering the English language in the 21st century.**

## Model Answer / Dialogue

**Fahim:** Hello Shanto! Good afternoon. What are you reading so attentively?

**Shanto:** Good afternoon, Fahim. I am reading an English newspaper editorial. To be honest, I often struggle with complex sentence structures and advanced vocabulary.

**Fahim:** I appreciate your dedicated effort, Shanto. Reading regularly is indeed the best way to conquer apprehension about English.

**Shanto:** You are right, Fahim. But sometimes I wonder why we must invest so much time and effort into learning a foreign language when we already have our sweet mother tongue, Bengali.

**Fahim:** Our mother tongue is undeniably our supreme pride and emotional heritage. However, in the modern interconnected world, English functions as the common global *lingua franca*. More than eighty percent of global information stored on computers and the internet is in English.

**Shanto:** That is a striking statistic! What about higher education?

**Fahim:** Almost all standard university textbooks, academic journals, research publications, and treatises on medicine, engineering, and artificial intelligence are composed in English. Without proficiency in English, pursuing reputable higher studies both at home and abroad becomes an uphill struggle.

**Shanto:** That makes total sense. And does it affect career prospects as well?

**Fahim:** Tremendously! International organizations, multinational corporations, software companies, and diplomatic missions invariably prioritize candidates who possess fluent communicative competence in English. It bridges geographical boundaries and opens doors to global employment.

**Shanto:** Thank you, Fahim! Your explanation has opened my eyes. From now on, I will practice speaking in English with you and enrich my vocabulary daily.

**Fahim:** You are most welcome, Shanto. Let us practice together every day after school!

## Key Points
- Distinction between mother tongue (identity) and global language (utility).
- Statistical dominance of English in academia, scientific research, and web data.
- Career prospects in multinational corporations and higher education.
