# Dialogue on Proper Use of Internet and Social Media

## Question / Topic
**Compose a dialogue between two classmates regarding the benefits and hazards of internet usage and how students should maintain digital balance.**

## Model Answer / Dialogue

**Arafat:** Hey Nabil, why do you look so fatigued today? Did you stay up late finishing the history project?

**Nabil:** Unfortunately, no, Arafat. I intended to study, but once I opened my phone to look up a reference, I fell into a rabbit hole of endless social media reels and viral videos. Before I knew it, midnight had passed!

**Arafat:** That is a very common trap, Nabil. Algorithm-driven platforms are engineered to capture our attention and trigger dopamine loops, making us lose track of precious time.

**Nabil:** I know, but isn't the internet essential for modern education? We cannot simply cut ourselves off from digital connectivity.

**Arafat:** Absolutely! The internet is arguably humanity's greatest encyclopedia. It gives us instant access to Khan Academy tutorials, Coursera courses, MIT lectures, open digital libraries, and collaborative coding repositories. The issue is not the technology itself, but our lack of intentional usage and self-discipline.

**Nabil:** Exactly. How do you manage to utilize the web without falling prey to digital distractions?

**Arafat:** I practice two simple strategies. First, I use website blockers and app timers that cap social media usage to twenty minutes a day. Second, when I sit down to study or write, I keep my phone in another room. I only use the web with a clear, predefined objective in mind.

**Nabil:** Those are actionable and practical guidelines! I will uninstall useless distracting gaming apps today and set up a study focus timer.

**Arafat:** That is the spirit! Technology should empower our dreams, not derail them.

**Nabil:** Thanks a lot for the wake-up call, Arafat!

## Useful Expressions
- *Fall into a rabbit hole:* Getting deeply engrossed in endless links or clips.
- *Intentional usage:* Using tools with a specific, conscious purpose.
