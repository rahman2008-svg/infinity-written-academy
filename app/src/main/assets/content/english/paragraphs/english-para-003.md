# A Rainy Day

## Paragraph
A rainy day is characterized by overcast skies, continuous downpours, cool breezes, and restricted outdoor human movement. From early morning, the horizon remains enveloped in thick, rolling dark clouds, obscuring the sun and casting a gloomy twilight hue over nature. Rain pours down incessantly—sometimes in soft, rhythmic drizzles and sometimes with thunderous claps and sudden flashes of lightning. Roads and low-lying alleys quickly become waterlogged, turning into muddy channels where vehicular traffic grinds to a crawl. For school students, a rainy day frequently heralds an unexpected holiday, allowing them to stay indoors, look out of misted window panes, read storybooks, and relish piping hot *khichuri* prepared affectionately by mothers. However, the scene presents a poignant contrast for daily wage earners, such as rickshaw-pullers, hawkers, and day-laborers, whose livelihood is paralyzed by the inclement weather. Having no indoor shelter or steady income, their families often face acute hunger and hardship. Thus, while a rainy day inspires romantic contemplation and artistic nostalgia among the privileged, it remains an ordeal of survival for the underprivileged toiling masses.

## Key Points
- Meteorological description: overcast skies, thunder, relentless showers.
- Urban & domestic life: holiday mood, warm dishes, waterlogging.
- Socio-economic contrast: romance vs. the struggle of poor laborers.
