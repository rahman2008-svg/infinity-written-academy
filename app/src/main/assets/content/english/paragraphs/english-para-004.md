# Early Rising

## Paragraph
Early rising is universally regarded as one of the most salutary and productive habits an individual can cultivate. It denotes leaving one's bed at the crack of dawn before sunrise. An early riser enjoys the immaculate serenity and crisp freshness of early morning air, which is rich in pure oxygen and free from urban toxic smog. Taking a brisk stroll or performing physical exercises amidst chirping birds and dew-drenched foliage revitalizes the body, improves cardiac health, and stimulates blood circulation. Furthermore, an early riser gains invaluable quiet hours before the distractions and clamor of the day commence. A calm and refreshed mind can grasp complex academic subjects with heightened focus and clarity. The famous age-old proverb rightfully encapsulates this wisdom: "Early to bed and early to rise, makes a man healthy, wealthy, and wise." In sharp contrast, a late riser always remains in a frantic rush, suffers from sluggishness, and invariably lags behind in the competitive race of life. Therefore, every student should strive diligently to form the habit of early rising from early childhood.

## Key Points
- Definition: waking up before dawn.
- Physiological perks: clean oxygen, morning walks, vigor.
- Academic & cognitive edge: serene hours, enhanced memorization.
