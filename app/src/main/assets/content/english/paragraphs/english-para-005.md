# Traffic Jam

## Paragraph
Traffic jam has emerged as one of the most frustrating, chronic, and paralyzing daily tribulations of modern urban life, especially in metropolitan centers like Dhaka and Chattogram. It occurs when a dense congestion of vehicles on roads brings vehicular movement to a prolonged standstill or an excruciatingly sluggish pace. The underlying causes of this ordeal are manifold: disproportionate population density, narrow roadways, inadequate road network infrastructure, illegal parking on busy avenues, and unauthorized roadside markets. Furthermore, the chaotic coexistence of slow-moving non-motorized vehicles (like rickshaws and push-carts) with high-speed buses and private cars, coupled with widespread violation of traffic lights by reckless drivers, exacerbates the bottleneck. The repercussions of traffic jams are catastrophic. Countless valuable working hours and fuel are squandered every day, students fail to reach schools and examination venues on time, and emergency ambulances carrying critically ill patients get trapped in gridlocks with fatal outcomes. To mitigate this unbearable menace, the authorities must strictly enforce automated traffic regulations, construct modern flyovers and metro rail corridors, eliminate roadside encroachments, and introduce efficient mass public transit systems.

## Key Points
- Causes: narrow roads, rickshaws alongside cars, indiscipline, illegal parking.
- Consequences: economic loss, wasted hours, patient emergencies.
- Solutions: metro rail, strict traffic law enforcement, mass transit.
