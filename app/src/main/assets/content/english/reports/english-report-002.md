# Science Fair Exhibition in College Campus

## Model Answer / Report

**Three-Day Inter-College Science Fair Concludes with Groundbreaking Innovations**

**Special Correspondent, Chattogram:**  
A vibrant three-day Inter-College Science Fair concluded yesterday at the spacious campus of City College, showcasing youthful scientific curiosity, engineering talent, and sustainable technological solutions.

The fair, themed "Science and Innovation for a Sustainable Future," was formally inaugurated on 5 March by the Vice-Chancellor of Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET). More than fifty reputed colleges from across the division actively participated, setting up over eighty interactive stalls and exhibits.

The young student researchers presented a wide spectrum of creative scientific projects spanning green renewable energy, artificial intelligence in agriculture, automated flood warning sensors, and solar-powered seawater desalination prototypes. Among the standout exhibits was an automated irrigation system built by Class XII students of City College, which utilizes soil moisture sensors and microcontrollers to economize agricultural water consumption by 40%. Another crowd-pulling invention was an eco-friendly drone engineered for detecting urban gas leakages.

Hundreds of enthusiastic visitors, teachers, and school pupils thronged the venue each day. On the final afternoon, an expert panel of professors evaluated the models based on scientific viability, originality, and cost-effectiveness. The top three winning teams were awarded laptops, medals, and research seed grants. In his closing address, the College Principal emphasized that cultivating such hands-on research among youth is indispensable for advancing the nation's technological sovereignty.
