**Ideal Model High School Celebrates Annual Prize Giving Ceremony with Grandeur**

**Staff Reporter, Dhaka:**  
The historic campus of Ideal Model High School wore a festive and celebratory look yesterday as the institution observed its Annual Prize Giving Ceremony 2026 amidst tremendous enthusiasm and youthful exuberance.

The formal proceedings commenced promptly at 10:00 a.m. with the recitation from the holy scriptures followed by the solemn singing of the National Anthem. The entire school auditorium was tastefully decorated with colorful festoons, flags, and floral motifs. The honorable Deputy Commissioner of Dhaka graced the momentous occasion as the Chief Guest, while eminent educationist Professor Dr. Aminul Islam attended as the Special Guest. The Headmaster of the school presided over the grand function.

The Headmaster presented the annual institutional report, highlighting the students' stellar performances in both academic board exams and national debates. Following the report, the Chief Guest delivered an inspiring speech, exhorting students to cultivate upright moral character, scientific temper, and patriotism alongside academic pursuits. 

The highlight of the event was the distribution of coveted trophies, medals, and certificates to over one hundred meritorious students for their outstanding accomplishments in academics, co-curricular arts, sports, and flawless attendance. The program culminated in an enthralling one-hour cultural show featuring patriotic choral songs, classical dance recitals, and an allegorical drama staged by the school drama club. The memorable day concluded at 4:30 p.m. with a vote of thanks from the Vice Principal.
