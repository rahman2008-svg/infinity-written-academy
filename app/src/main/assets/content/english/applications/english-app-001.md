# Application for Leave of Absence

## Question / Topic
**Write an application to the Headmaster of your school requesting leave of absence for three days on account of your sudden illness.**

## Application Writing Format
- Date: 12 March 2026
- Designation & School: To / The Headmaster / [Institution Name]
- Subject: Application for leave of absence
- Salutation: Sir,
- Opening sentence: Most respectfully and humbly...
- Cause & exact dates: From [Date] to [Date]
- Closing prayer: May I, therefore, pray and hope that...
- Subscription: Your most obedient pupil / Name, Class, Roll

## Model Answer / Application

12 March 2026  

To  
The Headmaster  
Ideal Model High School  
Motijheel, Dhaka  

**Subject: Application for leave of absence.**  

Sir,  

I have the honour to state that I am a bona fide student of Class X in your esteemed institution. I could not attend my regular classes from 9 March to 11 March 2026 as I was suddenly confined to bed with acute viral fever. The registered medical practitioner advised me to take complete physical rest for those three days.

I, therefore, earnestly pray and hope that your honour would be gracious enough to grant me the leave of absence for those three days only and oblige thereby.

I remain,  
Sir,  
Your most obedient pupil,  
**Nazmus Sakib**  
Class: X, Section: A  
Roll No: 04  

## Writing Tips
- Avoid punctuation marks after lines in modern block format address blocks.
- Keep the tone polite, dignified, and formally sound.
