# Application for Setting Up a School Library

## Question / Topic
**Write an application to the Headmaster on behalf of the students of your school requesting the establishment of a well-equipped modern school library.**

## Model Answer / Application

05 February 2026  

To  
The Headmaster  
Sonargaon Model High School  
Narayanganj  

**Subject: Prayer for setting up a well-equipped school library.**  

Sir,  

We, the undersigned students of your esteemed school, beg most respectfully to lay before you the following urgent prayer for your sympathetic consideration.

Ours is one of the most prominent educational institutions in the sub-district, showing enviable academic results every year. However, it is an undeniable misfortune that our school still lacks a dedicated, well-furnished library. A school library is universally acknowledged as the storehouse of wisdom and the intellectual powerhouse of an academic institution. In the absence of a library, students remain deprived of consulting reference books, journals, standard dictionaries, and creative literature. This absence severely limits our intellectual growth and critical thinking abilities. 

A modern library with a peaceful reading room would instill lifelong reading habits and foster healthy academic curiosity among all students.

We, therefore, earnestly pray and request that your honour would be kind enough to take immediate steps to establish a well-equipped school library on our school campus and oblige thereby.

We remain,  
Sir,  
Yours obediently,  
**The Students of Sonargaon Model High School**  
Represented by: Farhan, Class X, Roll 01
