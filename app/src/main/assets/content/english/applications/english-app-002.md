# Application for a Full-Free Studentship

## Question / Topic
**Write an application to the Principal of your college praying for a full-free studentship on ground of extreme financial hardship.**

## Model Answer / Application

18 January 2026  

To  
The Principal  
City College  
Chattogram  

**Subject: Prayer for a full-free studentship.**  

Sir,  

With humble submission, I beg to state that I am a regular student of Class XI (Science Group) in your renowned college. My father is the sole breadwinner of our seven-member family, working as a junior clerk with a meager monthly salary. Recently, due to his prolonged illness and substantial medical expenses, our family has been thrown into severe financial distress. Under these prevailing circumstances, it is utterly impossible for my father to defray the monthly tuition fees and educational expenses of my college education.

In this connection, I would like to bring to your kind notice that I secured GPA 5.00 in the SSC examination and have maintained a continuous attendance record of over 95% in college lectures. My profound eagerness to pursue higher studies will be cruelly nipped in the bud without benevolent institutional support.

I, therefore, fervently pray and hope that you would be benevolent enough to grant me a full-free studentship to enable me to continue my studies unhindered, for which act of kindness I shall ever remain grateful.

Yours faithfully,  
**Faisal Karim**  
Class: XI (Science)  
Roll No: 12
