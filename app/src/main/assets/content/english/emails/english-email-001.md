# Email Inviting a Friend to Birthday Celebration

## Question / Topic
**Write an email to your close friend inviting him to attend your upcoming 16th birthday celebration at your residence.**

## Email Structure
- **To:** recipient.email@example.com
- **Subject:** Crisp, friendly topic summary
- **Salutation:** Dear [Friend],
- **Opening:** Friendly greeting and purpose of the email
- **Details:** Date, time, venue, and planned activities
- **Closing:** Anticipation of presence & sign-off

## Model Answer / Email

**To:** tanvir.hasan24@gmail.com  
**Subject:** Invitation: Join me for my 16th birthday get-together on Sunday!  

Dear Tanvir,  

Hope you are doing great!  

You will be thrilled to know that my 16th birthday falls on this coming Sunday, 22 March. My parents have organized a cheerful evening get-together with a cozy dinner at our residence to mark the occasion. 

The gathering will commence at 6:30 p.m. at our rooftop garden. All our close school friends are coming, and we have planned some acoustic music, interactive board games, and great food. Your vivacious presence is indispensable to make the evening truly memorable for me. 

Please confirm your availability by Saturday evening. Looking forward to celebrating together!  

Warmest regards,  
**Shadman**  
Contact: 017XXXXXXXX
