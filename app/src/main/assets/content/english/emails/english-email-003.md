# Email Congratulating a Friend on Brilliant Success

## Question / Topic
**Send an email to your friend congratulating him on his outstanding achievement of securing GPA 5.00 with scholarship in the recent board examination.**

## Model Answer / Email

**To:** adnan.samir2026@gmail.com  
**Subject:** Hearty congratulations on securing Golden GPA 5.00!  

Dear Adnan,  

I was absolutely ecstatic to see the board examination results published on the official education portal this morning! Discovering your name among the top percentile with a glorious Golden GPA 5.00 filled my heart with boundless pride and happiness.

Your persistent hard work, perseverance, and disciplined study routine have rightfully earned this remarkable triumph. Throughout the year, I witnessed how diligently you prepared without being swayed by distractions. This splendid success has brought immense honour to your family and our entire alma mater.

Please accept my heartiest felicitations. Convey my respectful regards to your beloved parents, who must be overjoyed at your admirable feat. I look forward to meeting you soon to celebrate this memorable milestone.

With best wishes for your future endeavors,  
Yours warmly,  
**Mahmud**
