# Email Requesting Missed Class Assignments

## Question / Topic
**Send a formal email to your subject teacher requesting information about homework assignments and class notes missed during two days of sick leave.**

## Model Answer / Email

**To:** abdur.rahman.physics@academy.edu.bd  
**Subject:** Request for Missed Physics Assignments — Sifat Rahman (Class X, Roll 08)  

Respected Sir,  

Good day to you.  

I am Sifat Rahman, a student of Class X, Section B (Roll: 08). Due to severe seasonal influenza, I was unable to attend school on Monday and Tuesday (10 and 11 March). I have already submitted a formal medical absence note to the school administrative office today.

Could you kindly let me know the specific chapters covered in Physics during my absence, as well as any assigned numerical problem sets or laboratory assignments? I have already collected rough notes from my classmate Zubair and intend to complete all pending exercises by this coming Friday.

Thank you very much for your time and continued guidance.  

Respectfully yours,  
**Sifat Rahman**  
Class: X (Science), Roll: 08  
Ideal Model High School
