# Letter Advising Younger Brother to Read Newspaper

## Question / Topic
**Write a letter to your younger brother advising him on the immense importance of reading English daily newspapers regularly.**

## Model Answer / Letter

28 College Avenue  
Rajshahi  
10 February 2026  

Dear Nabil,  

At the outset of my letter, accept my warmest affection. Mother informed me in her recent phone call that you remain excessively immersed in memorizing text-books and pay little attention to contemporary global affairs. Hence, I feel compelled to share a piece of fraternal advice with you.

You must remember that textbook knowledge is merely the gateway to learning; real intellect flourishes through curiosity about the world around you. Reading a reputed daily newspaper every morning will widen your intellectual horizon. It keeps you abreast of current events, global politics, scientific discoveries, and economic trends. 

Moreover, as an ambitious student, regular reading of English dailies like 'The Daily Star' will dramatically enhance your vocabulary, comprehension ability, and grammatical intuition without painful rote memorization. Make it a daily ritual to spend at least twenty minutes perusing the editorial columns. 

I hope you will heed this sincere advice. Take care of your health and keep me updated about your quarterly exam results.

Yours affectionately,  
**Tanvir Bhai**

- **Abreast of:** Up to date with.
