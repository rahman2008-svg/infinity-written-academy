# Letter to a Friend about Preparation for Exam

## Question / Topic
**Write a letter to your friend describing your preparation for the upcoming Secondary School Certificate (SSC) examination.**

## Letter Writing Format
- **Heading:** Sender's Address & Date (Top-right or Top-left)
- **Salutation:** Dear [Friend's Name],
- **Opening Line:** Warm greetings and opening remarks
- **Body Paragraphs:** Detailed description of subject preparation and study routine
- **Closing Paragraph:** Inquiring about friend's studies & courteous regards to family
- **Subscription & Signature:** Yours ever / With love, [Your Name]
- **Envelope:** Standard address box with Stamp

## Model Answer / Letter

14 Green Road, Dhanmondi  
Dhaka-1205  
15 March 2026  

Dear Rafid,  

Take my cordial love. I received your letter yesterday and was delighted to learn that you are in good health. You wanted to know about my preparation for the ensuing Secondary School Certificate examination. Here is a brief account of it.

You will be pleased to know that my preparation has been progressing quite satisfactorily. I have already revised the entire syllabus of General Science, Mathematics, and Bengali twice. Previously, I felt somewhat diffident about English grammar and structured composition. To overcome this, I consulted our English teacher and have been solving past board question papers for two hours each morning. This consistent practice has significantly boosted my confidence.

Besides, I have prepared concise revision notes for physics, chemistry, and biology to facilitate quick revision on the eve of the examination. I strictly adhere to a disciplined daily schedule and regularly participate in weekly mock tests at our school. 

How is your preparation going on? Please write back to me soon. Convey my respectful regards to your respectable parents and affectionate love to your younger sister.

Yours ever,  
**Zubair**

## Key Points & Useful Vocabulary
- **Ensuing:** Upcoming / approaching.
- **Diffident:** Lacking self-confidence.
- **Facilitate:** Make easier.
- **Adhere to:** Strictly follow a rule or routine.

## Writing Tips
- Avoid overly colloquial slangs in examination letters; maintain a warm yet refined academic tone.
- Always include the envelope box at the end.
