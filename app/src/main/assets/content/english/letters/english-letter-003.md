# Letter to a Friend Describing a Picnic

## Question / Topic
**Write a letter to your intimate friend describing a delightful picnic that you recently enjoyed at a historical site.**

## Model Answer / Letter

Sector 7, Uttara  
Dhaka-1230  
22 January 2026  

Dear Sazzad,  

I hope you are enjoying splendid health and spirits. Your last letter reached me a week ago, but I could not reply immediately because I was on an educational excursion with my classmates. Today, I am excited to share the memorable experience of our picnic at Sonargaon.

On last Friday morning, around thirty students along with three respected teachers boarded our school bus at 7:00 a.m. The journey itself was exhilarating, filled with cheerful songs and witty recitations. Reaching Sonargaon within an hour and a half, we were captivated by the rich historical atmosphere of Panam Nagar. 

We explored the ancient brick mansions, the Folk Art and Crafts Museum, and the picturesque lakes. Our teachers explained the medieval trade history and traditional Jamdani weaving techniques of the region. At noon, we enjoyed a delicious meal of fragrant biryani and traditional desserts under the cool shade of ancient banyan trees. In the afternoon, we participated in friendly badminton matches and a brief cultural session.

We returned home safely at twilight with our hearts brimming with joyful memories. We genuinely missed your cheerful presence throughout the day.

Wishing you all the very best.

Yours ever,  
**Mahir**
