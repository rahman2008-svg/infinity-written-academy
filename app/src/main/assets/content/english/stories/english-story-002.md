# Where There Is a Will, There Is a Way

## Prompt / Theme
**Complete the story of a thirsty crow who flew across parched lands in search of water on a scorching summer afternoon.**

## Model Story

It was a blistering summer noon. The sun blazed fiercely in the cloudless sky like an incandescent furnace, and the earth lay parched and cracked. A solitary crow, exhausted from wandering across barren fields, was suffering from acute, agonizing thirst. His throat felt desiccated, and his wings grew heavier with every sluggish flap. He desperately surveyed the landscape for even a puddle of stagnant water, but all brooks, ponds, and ditches had evaporated under the merciless heat.

Just as utter exhaustion was about to overtake him, he spotted a tall clay pitcher resting on the veranda of an abandoned farmhouse. Overjoyed, he fluttered down and perched eagerly upon the pitcher's rim. 

Peering inside, his heart sank. There was indeed water at the very bottom of the vessel, but the neck of the pitcher was too narrow and the water level was far too low. Stretch his beak as much as he could, he could not reach even a single droplet.

A lesser creature might have surrendered in despair and perished. But the clever crow refused to concede defeat. He looked around intently and noticed a scatter of small, smooth pebbles lying in the courtyard dirt. An ingenious flash of inspiration struck his mind. 

Taking up a pebble in his beak, he dropped it carefully into the pitcher: *clink!* He flew back, picked up another, and dropped it in: *clink!* Patiently, persistently, pebble by pebble, he repeated this arduous labor. With each stone submerged, the water level inexorably climbed up the narrow neck. After dropping dozens of pebbles, the refreshing water finally reached the very brim! 

The crow dipped his beak gratefully, quenched his agonizing thirst with deep, cool gulps, and took flight into the blue expanse, rejuvenated and victorious.

## Moral of the Story
**Where there is a will, there is a way. Ingenuity and determination can conquer any difficulty.**

## Useful Vocabulary
- *Parched / Desiccated:* Severely dried out by heat.
- *Arduous:* Involving strenuous effort.
- *Inexorably:* Steadily and unstoppably.
